# Virtual Intelligence (AI)/Machine Learning (ML) use policy
(I will be using AI/ML interchangeably throughout this document. I frankly do not care about any potential nitpicky distinctions between the two as this is not an AI/ML project and this document is aimed at both technical and non-technical audiences.)

## TLDR:
- **This project does not use any AI.**
- If you use AI, please do not carelessly put any part of this project, including its documentation, into AI tools of any kind. (You can write your own summary/etc, I believe in you.)
- If you want to ethically use our data in machine learning (ex. for your research), we have some rules outlined below for how you may do so.

## What is AI?
Machine learning is, at its root, just a software program that can adapt itself to a given data set. Which means all AI needs data to be "trained" on.

Currently the kind of AI that a lot of companies try to push on consumers is ["unexplainable, dangerous, wasteful, stealing data, and [...] often confidently wrong"](https://www.realgoodai.org/what-we-do) (Real Good AI). 

It needs way too much data and processing power because it is not very efficient, meaning a lot of the data for the big AI tools was (and still is) procured by scraping data off the internet or other non-consentual means, instead of f.e. [creating their own custom training data (ex. CorridorKey (2026))](https://youtu.be/3Ploi723hg4?si=k7IEhRc2kAjshd8s) or using data in the public domain or with explicit permission.

## Ok cool, but why does that matter to the silly transgender sewing research project?

As a freely available data project, and with the rising normalisation of regular people casually using AI chatbots and AI tools companies force into our digital everyday, people will be tempted to use AI on our data.

**The queer community includes a lot of creatives** whose work and, in some cases, jobs have been stolen by data scraping and generative AI. In all likelihood, a large part of my own creative work is among that stolen data, despite having opted out of any AI use I had the ability to opt out of.

Beyond that, **our community's data can be highly sensitive**, notably for the trans people and crossdressers this project aims to serve, so it is important not to carelessly give it to big business and other institutions. 
Even just feeding something into a chatbot or AI assistant often means that data will be added to the data set that tool uses without the user's or data owner's informed consent or permission. If the company that runs it ever has a data leak that could become a massive problem for people whose data was included without their permission (ex. potentially outing them, identifying them to transphobic bad actors and organisations, etc).

Even though this project's data is largely kept anonymous, it contains identifyable data about me and my friends, and I still have a safeguarding and data protection duty to us, our participants and collaborators, anonymous or otherwise. **Publicly available/free to use under creative commons license/open-source does not mean public domain/copyright-free.**

At the same time I don't want to disable researchers and other projects with legitimate applications of machine learning that our results and survey data could be useful to from using it for good.

## Is AI used in this project?
**No.** No AI/ML has been/is/will be intentionally used in any part of this project. We aim to stay a 1 or below under the [REAL rating system](https://www.realgoodai.org/real-rating).

The project doesn't require machine learning to achieve what we are trying to do.

## Our stances:
- We do not condone AI that uses stolen data sets.
- We do not condone black box AI.
- There are many different kinds of AI/ML, including some genuinely useful and ethical ones. A lot of the mainstream AI the average consumer has access to are not that.

## If *you* use AI:
- I do not consent to my intellectual property, including any component of this project, being entered into third party or large-scale generative AI (genAI) tools (eg. chatGPT, Claude, AI image and video generators, etc)
    - This includes genAI tools built into your devices, online services and software you use (ex. "summarise ✨", AI assistants, etc)
    - Please do not cross the picket line if you are an ally or member of this community.

### If you want to use this data in your own research/project that involves ML
- You may use small-scale ML that wasn't trained on stolen data and does not feed data back to a third party data set.
    - This may include things like non-generative AI, custom-built, and locally/self-run software (including small-scale, custom-built, locally run generative AI).
    - In such cases, it is your own responsibility to optimise the data for your use.
- By using our data for your project, you are also subject to our [licensing](license.md). You must give credit to us as your data source and open access publish your own project/results under the same CC BY-SA license.
- Additionally, if you used our data, you must publicly declare how much and what kind of AI you used in your project, ideally including a [REAL rating](https://www.realgoodai.org/real-rating), for transparency.
